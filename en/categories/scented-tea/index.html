Vietnam

Vietnamese Jasmine Tea

Vietnamese jasmine tea is green tea scented with fresh jasmine blossoms. It is the everyday scented tea of Vietnam: jasmine is picked when its perfume peaks in the evening, layered with dried green tea, and traditionally about 1 kg of flowers scents 1 kg of tea. The result is sweet, clean and light, at home in a teapot or a glass of ice.

Vietnam

Vietnamese Lotus Tea

Vietnamese lotus tea is green tea scented with lotus. The traditional centre is Quảng An village on Hanoi’s West Lake, where makers use the hundred-petal Bach Diep lotus. The high-aroma method gathers the flower anthers, layers them with Thái Nguyên green tea and repeats scenting and drying for roughly 15–21 days; a simpler method stuffs tea into a fresh lotus overnight. The best cups are lightly astringent, sweet at the finish and cleanly floral.
