Uji tea area

West Lake tea area

A traditional Longjing tea area around West Lake in Hangzhou, known for the local Shi, Long, Yun, Hu and Mei labels and pan-fired processing. It is one traditional part of the Longjing protected area, not every flat green tea.

China · Fujian

Wuyi Mountains tea area

A core Wuyi rock-tea area in Fujian, known for Danxia cliffs, the Nine-Bend River, local tea populations and roasted oolong. “Zhengyan, Banyan and Zhoucha” are trade divisions, not statutory grades under the 2006 standard.

China · Henan

Xinyang tea area

A core green-tea area in Xinyang, Henan, known for Xinyang Maojian’s fine needle shape, chestnut aroma and fresh taste. Xinyang Maojian is a protected product; check origin, grade and process on the label.

China · Yunnan

Xishuangbanna tea area

A core Pu-erh tea area in southern Yunnan, known for old tea trees, tropical forest, large-leaf cultivars and sun-dried maocha. “Old tree” and “mountain” are material and ecology descriptions, not uniform quality grades.
