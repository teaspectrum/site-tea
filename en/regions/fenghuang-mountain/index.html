Fenghuang Mountain tea area

China · Guangdong

Fenghuang Mountain is the core of Guangdong Chaozhou dancong, at about 600–1,400 metres. Wudong, Dazhi and more than eighty aroma types produce honey-orchid, duck-shit and yellow-branch “one tree, one aroma” dancong.

Phoenix Dancong

Phoenix Dancong (Fenghuang Dancong) is a half-fermented oolong from the Phoenix Mountains of Chaozhou, Guangdong. It is made from the leaves, dormant buds and young shoots of Dancong lines, through withering, shaking, fixing, rolling and baking. The defining feature is aromatic individuality: honey-orchid, gardenia, magnolia, almond and cinnamon are sensory names for different lines, not botanical varieties.

Fenghuang dancong is “one tree, one aroma”: the high cloud of Chaozhou’s Fenghuang and Wudong mountains, the Fenghuang Shui Xian population, shaking and roasting produce more than eighty aromas such as honey-orchid, duck-shit, yellow-branch and orchid. Dancong is not one tea but a set of cultivars and aromas.

The short answer

Fenghuang Mountain lies in Chao’an, Chaozhou, Guangdong, with Fenghuang town, Wudong, Dazhi, Fengxi and Fengnan at the core; about 600–1,400 metres under a maritime climate with cloud and high humidity. The cultivar is Fenghuang Shui Xian population, divided by aroma into honey-orchid, duck-shit, yellow-branch, orchid, magnolia and cinnamon, over eighty types. Craft is shaking, kill-green, rolling and roasting, with heavy fire. Read by aroma and tree age first, then shaking and roast.

Geography and growing conditions

Fenghuang sits in northern Chaozhou on the Lianhua range at 600–1,400 metres, with about 2,000 mm of rain, cloud, high humidity and acidic soil; high gardens at Wudong and Dazhi have strong day–night range, slow growth and high aroma. Low gardens yield more but are less aromatic; “mountain rhyme” comes from altitude, soil, tree age and microclimate.

Wudong is the core, and ancient-tree dancong is expensive; gardens are mostly family-run with hand-plucking; typhoons, intense rain and frost are risks. Many bushes are a century or more old and are picked and made separately, so “one tree, one aroma” is cultivar and craft together.

History and industry

Fenghuang tea began in the Southern Song, and the Fenghuang Shui Xian population spread in the Ming–Qing; Chaozhou gongfu tea and dancong formed a complete culture of tea, vessel and brewing. State factories and dancong selection continued in the twentieth century, and after the 2000s domestic and specialty demand rose, with duck-shit aroma becoming famous.

Today family farmers, cooperatives and brands coexist, with dancong layered by aroma, tree age, mountain and fire; Wudong ancient trees are expensive, low-mountain dancong better value. The market saw duck-shit heat and rational return, with quality, mountain and fire central; tea tourism and gongfu culture support the region.

Cultivars and craft

Fenghuang dancong is the Fenghuang Shui Xian population, named by aroma: honey-orchid, duck-shit (silver flower), yellow-branch, orchid, magnolia, cinnamon, ginger-flower and night-blooming, over eighty. Craft runs plucking, withering, shaking, kill-green, rolling and roasting; shaking is the aroma core, roast has light, medium and heavy fire.

Shaking alternates shaking and spreading for edge oxidation and flower; rolling shapes strips and roasting stabilises aroma. Aroma depends on cultivar, altitude, tree age and shaking; duck-shit has a crude name but a silver-flower/orchid scent, honey-orchid is sweet, yellow-branch citrus-floral. Heavy fire is enduring and full, light fire aromatic.

Flavour and grades

Dancong is orange to golden, with high aroma and many types, mellow taste, sweet aftertaste and mountain rhyme; honey-orchid is sweet, duck-shit silver-floral, yellow-branch citrus, orchid elegant. Light fire is aromatic, heavy fire full and enduring; old bushes are softer with wood and moss.

Grade depends on aroma, tree age, mountain, shaking, fire and year; Wudong ancient trees are expensive, low-mountain cheaper. Buy by aroma, mountain, tree age and fire, not the “duck-shit” name alone; aroma is not quality, and craft and mountain matter.

Market and brewing

Fenghuang dancong sells mainly domestically, with Chaozhou, Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Beijing as key markets; duck-shit and honey-orchid are famous, with brands and farmers layered. Wudong ancient trees are expensive, low-mountain dancong better value; tea tourism and gongfu experiences support sales; e-commerce and livestreaming grow, but aroma and mountain claims need care.

Brew at 100°C for 5–10 seconds in gaiwan or zhuni pot, using 7–8 g per 120 ml; light fire quick, heavy fire can rest, old bushes can boil. Chaozhou gongfu uses three cups and “Guanyin patrols the city” and “Han Xin counts soldiers”. Visit Wudong and Fenghuang town in spring or autumn.

Myths and boundaries

“Dancong equals duck-shit” ignores over eighty aromas. “Duck-shit is low-end” ignores aroma and craft. “Dancong equals rock tea” ignores Guangdong Fenghuang and cultivar differences. “Aroma equals quality” ignores mountain, tree age and fire. “Dancong is black tea” ignores oolong craft.

Fenghuang is a region and aroma system, not one tea; aroma, tree age, mountain, shaking and fire together define it. This page gives structure; tea pages and producers carry specifics.
