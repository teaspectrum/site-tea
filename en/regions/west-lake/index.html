West Lake tea area

China · Zhejiang

West Lake Longjing is a flat green tea from around Hangzhou’s West Lake. Shifeng, Meijiawu, Longwu and Wengjiashan grow Longjing 43 and local population, with ten hand-firing motions producing green colour, rich aroma, sweet taste and beautiful shape.

West Lake Longjing is not a synonym for “Hangzhou green tea” but a small area around West Lake: Shifeng, Meijiawu, Longwu, Wengjiashan and Manjuelong use Longjing 43 and local population, with spreading, kill-green and ten hand-firing motions to make flat, straight, green and aromatic “four excellences”.

The short answer

West Lake Longjing comes from Hangzhou’s West Lake scenic area and surroundings, with core sub-areas “Shi, Long, Yun, Hu, Mei”: Shifeng, Longjing, Yunqi, Hupao and Meijiawu; about 100–300 metres, lake-mountain mist and acidic red soil. Longjing 43 and local population dominate; craft is spreading, kill-green, rolling and final firing, with ten hand motions. Read by area and season first, then cultivar, hand-firing and grade.

Geography and growing conditions

The core sits between the West Lake hills and the Qiantang River, low in altitude but with lake-mountain mist, high humidity and moderate day–night range; red, acidic, well-drained soil. The lake and hills create microclimates, and Shifeng, Meijiawu, Longwu and Wengjiashan differ; slow spring warming gives fine pre-Qingming and stronger pre-rain tea.

Core area is limited, and price differs greatly from surrounding Fuyang, Xiaoshan and Yuhang; the GI protects the “West Lake Longjing” name. Late cold, frost and plucking labour are risks, and core gardens face tourism and land pressure.

History and industry

West Lake Longjing was recorded in the Tang, produced at Baiyun Peak in the Song, became famous in the Ming, and in the Qing Qianlong’s “eighteen imperial bushes” made it a national tea. In the twentieth century, Longjing 43 clones spread and machine processing increased; West Lake Longjing became a state gift and brand.

Today core farmers, cooperatives and brands coexist, with hand and machine processing; pre-Qingming is expensive, pre-rain better value. GI, brand protection and anti-counterfeiting are central; core output is limited, and surrounding and blended tea fills the market, so buy by mark and origin.

Cultivars and craft

West Lake plants Longjing 43 and local population; Longjing 43 is early, even and aromatic, population is deeper and more enduring. Craft runs spreading, first firing (kill-green and shaping), resting and final firing (drying and shaping), with ten hand motions: grab, shake,搭, press, push, knock, flick, grind and press; machines use straightening and drying.

Hand-firing makes flat, straight, smooth and green leaf: high heat and light motion first, low heat and heavier motion final; spreading removes green notes, resting evens moisture. Pre-Qingming picks one bud with one opening leaf, pre-rain one bud with two leaves; hand and machine styles differ, hand more aromatic, machine more even.

Flavour and grades

Dry tea is flat, straight, smooth and emerald; liquor clear green, aroma bean, chestnut or orchid, taste fresh and sweet, leaf bright green. Pre-Qingming is fine and aromatic; pre-rain is stronger and more enduring; population is deeper, Longjing 43 more aromatic.

Grade depends on area, season, bud, colour, aroma, liquor and leaf; Shifeng, Meijiawu and Longwu are expensive, and core versus surrounding differs widely. Buy by GI, origin, season and hand/machine, not the “West Lake Longjing” pack alone.

Market and brewing

West Lake Longjing sells mainly domestically, with gifts, pre-Qingming and brand premiums; core output is limited, and surrounding and blended tea is common. Tea tourism, West Lake culture and international gifts support sales; anti-counterfeit marks and origin proof are key.

Brew at 80–85°C in glass or gaiwan for one to two minutes; rinse first, then pour high, avoiding heat that cooks the leaf. Use about 3 g per 150 ml; pre-Qingming cooler and quicker, pre-rain slightly hotter. Visit Shifeng, Meijiawu and Longwu in spring, especially pre-Qingming; village experiences need booking.

Myths and boundaries

“West Lake Longjing equals all Hangzhou Longjing” ignores core and surrounding areas. “Pre-Qingming is always better” ignores taste and endurance. “Hand-made is always better than machine” ignores skill. “Longjing 43 is the only cultivar” ignores local population. “Flat equals Longjing” ignores other flat teas.

West Lake is a region and brand system, not one tea; area, season, cultivar, hand-firing and grade together define it. This page gives structure; tea pages and producers carry specifics.
