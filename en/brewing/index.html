Gongfu brewing

A brewing style that uses a small pot or gaiwan, a relatively high leaf ratio and repeated short infusions. It is common in southern Fujian, Chaozhou and Taiwan, but it is not the only refined way to brew tea.

Water temperature

Water temperature changes how quickly aroma, sweetness and bitterness are extracted. It is one of the easiest variables to adjust, but it should be changed together with leaf ratio and infusion time.
