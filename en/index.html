Longjing tea

Longjing is a pan-fired green tea from the Hangzhou area, known for its flat, straight leaves and fresh, fragrant taste. The geographical indication standard defines its protected area, suitable cultivars and processing requirements; West Lake’s traditional labels are not the same as statutory grades.

China · Fujian

Da Hong Pao

Da Hong Pao is a flagship Wuyi rock-tea product name. GB/T 18745-2006 groups Wuyi rock tea into Da Hong Pao, Shuixian, Rougui, Mingcong and Qizhong; commercial Da Hong Pao is usually a blend, and the mother trees are protected rather than picked for sale.

China · Yunnan

Raw Pu-erh

Raw Pu-erh (sheng pu'er) is one of the two product types of geographical-indication Pu-erh: Yunnan large-leaf sun-dried tea, steamed, pressed and dried, without pile fermentation. It starts bright, floral and bitter, then changes with storage — but ageing is a storage outcome, not a standard guarantee.
